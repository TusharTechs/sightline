Sintel was chosen deliberately. The hackathon rules bar third-party trademarks
and copyrighted material from demo videos, and a Creative Commons open movie
satisfies that cleanly. It is also the right test case on the merits: real
dialogue, real score, real motion — none of which a synthetic fixture provides.

Attribution must appear in any demo video that uses this footage.
